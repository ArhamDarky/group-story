# group-story

Once upon a time, a robot and a wizard were chatting in a cozy coffee shop when an alien and a cowboy walked in. Curious about each other, they all sat together and started talking over drinks.

They thought they were sipping coffee, but after a few sips, they realized something was off — it was actually hot chocolate. Everyone paused, looked at their mugs, and just started laughing.

From that day on, they never went back to coffee. Hot chocolate became their go-to. And honestly? They never looked back. The End.
